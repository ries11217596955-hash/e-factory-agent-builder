## 3. Main Gap Found

The archive still frames Agent Builder mainly as:

```text
self-building factory for AI agents
```

That is useful, but incomplete after the Owner's clarification.

The missing active doctrine was:

```text
Agent Builder is not “LLM + tools”.
Agent Builder must grow as a primitive artificial brain cell:
self-observing, self-changing, self-verifying, and capable of growing task-specific child forms.
```

Without this doctrine, the assistant may drift back into the wrong solution:
- connect a ready LLM;
- call it the brain;
- treat tools as hands;
- skip the deeper goal of growing internal logic.

## 4. Design Decision

Do not replace the route lock.

Do not rewrite everything.

Use a controlled settings evolution:

```text
keep route
keep proof law
keep material governance
add brain-cell doctrine
merge key active files
replace short instructions V2 with V3
```
